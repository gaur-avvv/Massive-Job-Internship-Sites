# 🎓 Student Job & Internship Portal Guide

Welcome to the ultimate directory for software engineering, artificial intelligence, tech, and core development opportunities! This guide is curated to help students land high-impact internships and full-time roles across top tech companies, startups, and open-source ecosystems.

---

## 🚀 Top Job & Internship Platforms

### 1. General & High-Volume Tech Job Boards
* **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/):** Connect directly with university recruiters, follow company updates, and apply to early-career engineering programs.
* **[Unstop (formerly Dare2Compete)](https://unstop.com/):** Ideal for tech hackathons, hiring challenges, coding competitions, and campus recruitment drives.
* **[Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)](https://wellfound.com/):** Direct access to high-growth tech startups and AI labs where you can connect directly with founders and CTOs.
* **[Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/):** Search early-career roles while reviewing interview experiences, compensation figures, and company cultures.
* **[Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/):** High-volume aggregator for regional, remote, and global software development listings.

---

### 2. Developer-Focused & Early-Career Tech Hubs
* **[GitHub Careers & Education](https://education.github.com/):** Access Student Developer Pack perks, open-source programs, and student recruitment pools.
* **[GeeksforGeeks Job Portal](https://practice.geeksforgeeks.org/jobs):** Direct hiring tracks, off-campus drives, and campus recruitment tests tailored for CS students.
* **[LeetCode Jobs](https://leetcode.com/jobs/):** Coding-centric job boards featuring organizations that hire directly through technical challenge evaluations.
* **[HackerRank Jobs](https://www.hackerrank.com/jobs):** Assessments and direct interview invitations based on standardized skill scores.

---

### 3. Open-Source & Global Student Programs
* **[Google Summer of Code (GSoC)](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/):** Paid global program pairing student developers with open-source organizations for 12+ week coding projects.
* **[MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io/):** 12-week internship alternative where students contribute to real-world software under expert mentorship.
* **[LFX Mentorship](https://lfx.linuxfoundation.org/tools/mentorship/):** Open-source mentorship opportunities across Linux Foundation projects (Kubernetes, PyTorch, Cloud Native).
* **[Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/):** Remote internships supporting underrepresented groups in tech through open-source contributions.

---

## 💡 Pro-Tips for Landing Your Role

1. **Optimize Your Resume:** Keep your resume concise, metric-driven, and tailored toward specific tech stacks (e.g., Python, React, FastAPI, Docker, PyTorch).
2. **Build Public Proof-of-Work:** Host complete, deployed projects on GitHub with comprehensive `README.md` documentation, architecture diagrams, and live demo links.
3. **Engage in Open Source:** Consistent contributions to recognized open-source repositories significantly stand out to tech recruiters over plain coursework.
4. **Leverage Referral Networks:** Reach out directly to alumni and senior engineers on LinkedIn with a brief, personalized message highlighting relevant projects.
